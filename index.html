Easy living on the edge of the trail.

Salem Place is a five-story elevator condominium with private balconies, a first-floor garage and the Northern Strand rail trail right next door, minutes from Route 1, the T, Revere Beach and Boston.

The Building

A well-kept community of 40 homes

Salem Place offers spacious one- and two-bedroom condominiums in a quiet elevator building, most of them two-bedroom, two-bath homes. Every home has a private balcony. Many units have deeded parking spaces, including spaces in the building's first-floor garage, and there is guest parking for visitors. The building backs onto open green space.

Right next door

The Northern Strand Community Trail

Step outside and you're on the Northern Strand, a paved rail trail of about 10 miles linking Everett, Malden, Revere, Saugus and Lynn. Walk, run or bike through wooded marshland, ride toward Lynn and the ocean, or head the other way toward the Mystic River and Boston's path network.

Walk, run, bike

The trail runs right along the back of the property. Car-free miles, steps from your door.
