Parking

How parking works at Salem Place, the parking rules, and the spaces assigned to each unit.

How parking works

  • Salem Place has covered parking in the first-floor garage and outdoor spaces.
  • Under the Master Deed, parking spaces were conveyed to individual units as exclusive easements. An assigned space is for the exclusive use of the unit it belongs to, and no one else may park there without that owner's permission.
  • Spaces that were never deeded to a unit belong to the Condominium Trust. The Trustees may assign them to an owner for a period and charge they set, with the money going to the association. Unassigned spaces are available for occasional use by residents and guests under the rules.
  • Spaces are for registered private passenger vehicles of the people who live in the building. Boats, commercial trucks and other vehicles or items need the Trustees' prior written permission.
  • The association maintains and repairs the parking areas, including assigned spaces, as a common expense.

Parking rules

From the Rules & Regulations, Section I.

  • All owners, residents and visitors must follow all parking rules.
  • The speed limit throughout the Salem Place parking lot is 5 miles per hour.
  • No vehicle may park in another person's assigned or numbered space without permission. Blocking anyone's access to or from their space is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate towing.
  • No vehicle may block building exits or fire lanes.
  • All vehicles must be registered and licensed.
  • Visitors must park in the spaces marked “Visitor/Guest”: the 5 spaces facing the street and 1 in the main lot near the light.
  • Entering or exiting the garage through the wrong door is prohibited and subject to a fine. The entrance and exit doors are clearly marked with arrows. For safety, close the door immediately after entering.
  • All vehicles must be moved for snow removal. If you will be away in winter, consider leaving a key with a neighbor. Vehicles not moved are subject to a fine or towing. If you do not have an assigned space, park on the gravel.
  • Vehicles on the gravel must always leave room for trash pickup, especially during snowstorms.
  • Recreational vehicles and equipment, including bicycles, mopeds, scooters and skis, are not allowed on the premises.
