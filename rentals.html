The homes

One- and two-bedroom condominiums, from about 680 to 1,280 square feet, all with private balconies, in a five-story elevator building.

Parking

Many units have deeded parking spaces, including spaces in the first-floor garage, plus designated visitor spaces for guests. Ask the listing agent which space comes with the unit.

Location

292 Salem Street, Revere, Massachusetts
Salem Place sits in North Revere, a quiet, residential corner of the city with tree-lined streets, friendly neighbors and a true small-town feel. It's right on the Northern Strand rail trail and minutes from Route 1, the T, Revere Beach and Boston.

Units are rented directly by their owners or through their agents. The Salem Place Condominium Trust does not manage rentals, set rents or screen tenants. Tenants must follow the building's Rules & Regulations.
